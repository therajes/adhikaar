# Privacy model

Receivers do not register, connect a wallet, or disclose a name. The raw citizen challenge remains on-device. Backend analytics are limited to hashed/session-scoped identifiers, coarse results, and timing metadata. The application records no call audio, contact list, raw IP address, OTP, PIN, CVV, password, or payment instruction.

The chain stores only hashes and governance events. Safe callbacks are fictional public records in this prototype. Production deployments must complete data-protection review, define retention, and publish institution-specific notices.
